# Excel_Sales_Dashboard

1.Project :Name Excel_Sales_Dashboard

Interactive Excel Sales Dashboard using Pivot Tables and Charts to analyze sales performance, revenue, products, regions, salespersons, commissions, and yearly trends from 2013–2023 sales data.

This project is an interactive **Sales Dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel** using a dataset of more than **24,000 sales transactions**. The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, product revenue, regional sales, salesperson commissions, units sold, and revenue trends through Excel-based data analysis and visualization.

The raw dataset contains **24,268 sales records** with important fields including Product Code, Product Name, Sales Region, Sales Representative, Transaction Date, Order ID, Store ID, Units Sold, Price Per Piece, Total Amount, Total Commission, and Total Revenue. The data was organized and analyzed using **Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts** to convert detailed transaction-level information into useful business insights.

The first analysis focuses on **product-wise revenue performance**. The Pivot Table compares the total revenue generated by different products. CPU generated the highest total revenue, followed by LCD and Touchscreen, while Card Reader generated the lowest revenue among the listed products. The overall total revenue from the product analysis is approximately **733.11 million**.

The project also includes **regional sales analysis**. Total sales amounts are compared across East, North, South, and West regions. The North region generated the highest sales amount, followed by East, South, and West. The total sales amount across all regions is approximately **771 million**. This analysis helps identify differences in regional performance.

Another important section analyzes **salesperson commission performance**. The Pivot Table shows the total commission earned by each sales representative and also identifies the maximum commission amount. Mohit Dhinghra has the highest total commission in the analysis, followed by Rajan Kishore and Rajkishor Gupta. The maximum individual commission value recorded is **10,380**.

The project also analyzes **units sold by product**. This helps compare product demand and identify which products have the highest sales volume. Touchscreen has the highest number of units sold in the Pivot Table, followed by Wifi Router, LCD, External Hard Drive, and Wireless Keyboard & Mouse. The total units sold across the analyzed dataset are approximately **6.12 million**.

A separate Pivot Table provides **year-wise maximum revenue analysis** from 2013 to 2023. This allows the user to compare the highest revenue transaction recorded in each year and understand revenue patterns over time.

The final **Dashboard sheet** brings the important analysis together into a visual and easy-to-understand format. Charts, tables, and dashboard elements help users quickly interpret product performance, regional sales, salesperson commissions, units sold, and revenue trends without manually reviewing thousands of records.
